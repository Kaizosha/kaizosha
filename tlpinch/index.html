02 One gesture to the point

Keep the meaning.
Lose the length.

TL;Pinch meets you inside Safari. On touch devices, pinch inward on the page. On Mac, open it from the Safari toolbar.

  1. 01
    Read

    Stay on the article, guide, or long read already open in Safari.

  2. 02
    Choose the scope

    Pinch at default zoom for the whole page, or highlight a passage and then open TL;Pinch.

  3. 03
    Understand

    Get a short answer and the supporting ideas that make it useful.

  4. 04
    Keep reading

    Close the result and continue exactly where you left off.

03 Automatic intent

The page decides
what comes next.

Open TL;Pinch with nothing selected and it summarizes. Highlight text first and it explains that passage in context.

01 / Whole page

02 / Selected text

Understand what it means.

The selected passage is explained against the nearby text, not pulled out of context.

05 Set up on each platform

Enable it.
Choose the sites.

  1. 01Open Safari settings

    Use Settings on iPhone or iPad, or Safari Settings on Mac.

  2. 02Open Extensions

    Find the Extensions section for Safari.

  3. 03Turn on TL;Pinch

    Find the extension and enable it.

  4. 04Choose website access

    Allow TL;Pinch on the sites where you want to use it.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 26, or macOS 26, on an Apple Intelligence-capable device with Apple Intelligence enabled, ready, and available for the device language.
