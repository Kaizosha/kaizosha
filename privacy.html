Privacy

Kaizōsha builds and sells software — apps and websites. We care how that software handles data.

For our own sites and apps: we collect only what we need to run them and to respond to you (e.g. when you contact support). We do not sell your data. We may use cookies or similar tech where necessary; we keep things minimal.

For software we sell or build for others: each product may have its own data practices. Where we host or operate it, we follow the same principle: minimal collection, no selling of personal data. Where you or a client hosts it, you control the data.

If you have questions about your data or our software, see contact.
