02 About

Software with
a point of view.

Kaizō Konpaku works across native Apple software, on-device intelligence, realtime captions and translation, Safari extensions, open-source frameworks, developer utilities, and focused web experiences.

Through Kaizōsha, he develops products that keep complex technology close to the person using it: native when possible, private by architecture, and direct in how they communicate.

His public GitHub work appears under the KaizoKonpaku account and the Kaizōsha organization.
