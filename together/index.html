02 From video to understanding

One video.
Every word.

Open a video, choose the languages, and let Together build captions while you watch. Keep the original, show the translation, or see both at once.

  1. 01Choose

    Pick a video from Photos or Files, or open it directly in Together.

  2. 02Set

    Choose the spoken language, translation, caption mode, and appearance.

  3. 03Watch

    Play with native controls as on-device captions appear in context.

  4. 04Export

    Save original, translated, or bilingual captions as VTT, SRT, or text.
