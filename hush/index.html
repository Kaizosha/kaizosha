03 Memory, on your terms

Keep the context.
Control the context.

Hush can carry selected memories between sessions, while local chat history keeps prior work nearby. Each memory can be enabled, disabled, inspected, or removed.

  • 01 Save only what should persist
  • 02 See which memories are active
  • 03 Start a clean session at any time

04 A clear boundary

Your Mac first.

Capture and session management begin on your Mac. Your Gemini key is stored in Keychain. When you ask for AI analysis, the selected context is sent to Google’s Gemini service using your key.

Hush never makes consent automatic. Record and capture only when you have permission, and review sensitive context before you send it.
